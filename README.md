The dashboard was developed using Power BI and includes multiple data sources that have been integrated to provide an accurate and comprehensive view of Atlas Labs' human resources. It is designed to be interactive, enabling dynamic analysis so users can easily explore data across multiple sections and details. The dashboard is based on key KPIs, such as the attrition rate, total active employees, and monthly hiring trends, making it a powerful tool for data-driven decision-making.

Key Findings and Problem Solutions:

Attrition Rate: The dashboard shows a general attrition rate of 16.1%, which is relatively high, especially in the sales department, where the rate reaches 40%. A potential solution here is to review the department's work policies, such as improving the salary structure or providing a more attractive work environment.

Employee Distribution by Department: With 828 employees in the technology department and 354 in sales, it is clear that technology is the company’s main driver. However, the sales department needs additional support to enhance performance and reduce the burden on current employees, which could help lower the attrition rate.

Monthly Hiring Trends: The dashboard reveals an irregular hiring pattern throughout the year, with peaks in the early months. Sustainable and continuous hiring plans could be proposed to avoid pressure during certain periods and distribute workloads more evenly throughout the year.

Employee Distribution by Age Group and Marital Status: Most employees are in the 30–40 age group and are married. Work-life balance programs can be enhanced for these employees to help increase job satisfaction and reduce attrition.

Salary Analysis by Ethnicity: There is a disparity in average salaries across different ethnic backgrounds. To ensure job fairness, salary policies could be reviewed to guarantee equal opportunities, promoting a fair and inclusive work environment.

Attrition by Travel and Overtime: The dashboard indicates that employees who travel frequently have a higher attrition rate (24.9%). Attrition could be reduced by offering additional support to these employees, such as reducing business trips and providing extra compensation.

Technical Aspects:

Data Integration: The data was connected from HR systems via direct connection to Power BI services, ensuring continuous data updates and real-time analysis.

Advanced Analytics: The dashboard relies on advanced DAX calculations to measure various KPIs and provide accurate insights into employee details, enabling in-depth analysis for each department and indicator separately.

Customization: The dashboard offers extensive customization capabilities, allowing users to filter and analyze data based on department, marital status, travel, and other criteria, enhancing flexibility and accuracy in analysis.

Dynamic Visualizations: The use of dynamic and interactive charts enhances the ability to make quick comparisons and identify instant trends, supporting decision-makers in making quick, informed decisions.

Dashboard Importance for Decision-Makers:

The dashboard provides a reliable and flexible platform that enables data-backed decisions, contributing to improved hiring and retention policies and increased employee satisfaction.
